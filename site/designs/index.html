Five designs

Five designs of the home page, each with the same words.

  1. The ticket

    The live home page as it is now: warm paper, serif headings and numbered steps.

  2. The blueprint

    Dark blue drawing paper with a faint grid, monospace labels and the six steps drawn as boxes and arrows.

  3. The timeline

    An editorial page in serif type, with the timestamps set large as the spine and the steps hung off it.

  4. The evidence

    A terminal view: the request file, the 43 seconds it took to make the repository, and the rest as measured facts.

  5. The poster

    One strong colour field, one large line, and the three pages of the guide as giant numerals.
