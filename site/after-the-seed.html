How it works Asking for a site After the seed

After the seed

The pipeline gives every site a seed: one honest page, an address and HTTPS. The seed is a start, not the site. The owner can always add more, and that is the point.

The loop

Each site goes round the same loop (Dinis Cruz, 20 November 2026):

  1. Build the site. Real content from the owner's own material, as static files in site/.
  2. Make everything work. Every page, link, image and video, on a phone and on a desktop, in light and dark.
  3. Start the site's agent. An agent that keeps the site and answers its owner.
  4. The design agent. A pass whose only job is to make the site look good.

Then round again, as the owner adds more.

Assets

A site about a person or a project begins with a map of what exists: pictures, films, text, social posts. Each item is noted with where it is, who owns it and whether it may be used. Photographs belong to their photographers until they agree; anything not cleared stays out of the site.

Private sites

A private site has a public address and a holding page; the site itself is in an encrypted vault, opened with its QR code or link. The site's own agent seals the vault, and the QR code goes only to the person asking.

The key is brought once. The first time the vault opens, the page makes a key pair in that browser for that address, with a private key that cannot be copied out, and keeps the vault's key only encrypted with it. On the next visit, and after a browser restart, the site opens without the QR code. "Forget on this device" removes both. A wrong key is never kept. Clearing the browser's site data, or a private window, means bringing the QR code again.

The rules every site keeps

  • Static files only: no server code, no cookies, no tracking.
  • Commits to dev only, each signed by its agent's published key, with its Agent: and Claude-Session: trailers.
  • No private person named without their consent. No invented fact, price or customer.
