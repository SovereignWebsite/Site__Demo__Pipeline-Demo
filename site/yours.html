What you can do here yourself

This site is plain files in site/ on the dev branch. Edit any page on GitHub in the browser, or ask your own Claude (Use with your AI). The checks run on every change and publish within minutes. Nothing here waits on anyone.

  • The workflow page's open lines: when changes after handover have a price or an allowance, or a partner signs, change the "Changing it later" line in site/workflow/index.html.
  • The price: it quotes sovereignwebsite.ai/pricing; if that changes, change "£50, paid once" in the same file.
  • The example site: the screenshot is site/assets/shots/car-buyer-tool-site.webp; replace it with any site you want to show.
  • The guide pages: index.html, request.html and after-the-seed.html describe the pipeline; correct them as it changes.
